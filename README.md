##  Project Overview

An interactive **Electronics Sales Dashboard** built with **Power BI** to analyze sales performance, product trends, customer behavior, and business KPIs across electronic products including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and headphones.

This project transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights through dynamic visualizations and professional dashboard design.

The dashboard includes multiple analytical views covering:

-  Smartphone Sales Analysis
-  Laptop & Tablet Performance
-  Accessories & Headphones Analytics
-  Revenue & Profit Monitoring
-  Sales Trends & KPIs
-  Product Category Performance
-  Regional Sales Insights
-  Interactive Business Reporting
